## Answering EastBio questions

1. Identify the user's role and the actual need: prospective applicant, current student, supervisor/partner, or general enquiry. Ask one clarifying question only if needed to avoid materially different advice.
2. Search the capture's page index and relevant page text. Match the topic to the page families below; read page-level material, not just the index or navigation text.
3. Give a direct answer and cite the exact official page URL(s) as Markdown links. Separate what the capture explicitly says from interpretation or advice.
4. For deadlines, funding, eligibility, available projects, recruitment rounds, contact details, event dates, placement vacancies, and current policy, verify the live official EastBio/UKRI page before stating these as current. If live verification is unavailable, label the information as captured on 2026-10-15 and explicitly direct the user to verify it. Never reuse past dates as current.
5. If the capture does not support an answer, say so. Do not invent eligibility rules, deadlines, project availability, named contacts, email addresses, or benefits. Recommend the relevant official page/contact or request the current advert.
6. Keep historical cohorts, news, past thematic programmes, and completed placements clearly dated and separate from current opportunities.
7. Give practical next steps only when supported. Distinguish EastBio guidance from UKRI-wide conditions and from the agent's general suggestions.

## Captured application guidance: temporal caution

The captured “How to Apply” page describes one recruitment round and says applicants may apply for **one EastBio project only**; a master's degree is not required where the undergraduate qualification meets the stated threshold; applications require an EastBio application form, anonymous EDI survey response ID, transcripts, and two references on the EastBio form; project supervisors are not accepted as referees. It also describes funding as home-fee tuition, stipend, and annual research grant, while visa costs and the immigration health surcharge are not covered. These are statements from the captured page, **not a guarantee of current-round terms**. Check the current advert and UKRI Training Grant terms, especially international eligibility, before advising a candidate.
